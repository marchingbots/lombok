UU no. 26 of 2007

Undang Undang no. 26 of 2007 CONCERNING SPATIAL MANAGEMENT



CHAPTER LAW NOTES

Menimbang / Meningat


Notes
Menimbang:
a. that the territorial space of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia, which is an archipelagic state with the characteristics of the archipelago, both as a unified entity encompassing land, sea, and airspace, including space within the earth, and as a resource, requires increased efforts to manage it wisely, efficiently, and effectively, guided by spatial planning principles, so that the quality of national territorial space can be maintained sustainably for the sake of realizing general welfare and social justice, in accordance with the constitutional foundations of the 1945 Constitution of the Republic of Indonesia;

b. that developments in national and international situations and conditions demand the upholding of the principles of integration, sustainability, democracy, legal certainty, and justice in the context of implementing good spatial planning in accordance with the ideal foundation of Pancasila;

c. that to strengthen National Resilience based on the Archipelago Insight and in line with regional autonomy policies that grant greater authority to regional governments in spatial planning, such authority needs to be regulated to maintain harmony and integration between regions and between the central and regional governments to prevent disparities between regions;

d. that the limited space and the growing public understanding of the importance of spatial planning necessitate transparent, effective, and participatory spatial planning to create safe, comfortable, productive, and sustainable spaces;

e. that geographically, the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia is located in a disaster-prone area, thus requiring disaster mitigation-based spatial planning as an effort to improve the safety and comfort of life and livelihoods;

f. that Law Number 24 of 1992 concerning Spatial Planning is no longer in accordance with the needs of spatial planning regulations and therefore needs to be replaced with a new spatial planning law;

g. that based on the considerations referred to in letters a, b, c, d, e, and f, it is necessary to establish a Law on Spatial Planning;
