Ministry Regulations:

PM No. 18 of 2024 ATR/BPN
(implementation of land development)
Bahasa Version English Translation Breakout and Notes
a. that in order to realize optimal utilization of land and space, increase efficiency, effectiveness, value of benefits and productivity of land and space use in accordance with spatial planning to support sustainable development, it is necessary to organize land development;
b. that in order to meet the land needs of the community, land utilization, increase investment, improve regional economics, and improve land management, guidelines for implementing land development are required;
c. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a and b, it is necessary to stipulate a Regulation of the Minister of Agrarian Affairs and Spatial Planning/Head of the National Land Agency concerning the Implementation of Land Development;

PM No. 9 of 2022 ATR/BPN
(Amendments to the Regulation of the Minister of Agrarian Affairs and Spatial Planning/Head of the National Land Agency Number 15 of 2021 concerning Coordination of Spatial Planning Implementation)
Bahasa Version English Translation Breakout and Notes
a. that in order to realize transparent, effective, participatory, productive and sustainable spatial planning, a Spatial Planning Forum is required;
b. that for the effective operationalization of the Spatial Planning Forum, it is necessary to change the provisions in the Regulation of the Minister of Agrarian Affairs and Spatial Planning/Head of the National Land Agency Regulation Number 15 of 2021 concerning Coordination of Spatial Planning Implementation;
c. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a and b, it is necessary establish the Regulation of the Minister of Agrarian Affairs and Spatial Planning Room/Head of the National Land Agency regarding Amendments to the Regulation of the Minister of Agrarian Affairs and Spatial Planning Room/Head of National Land Agency Number 15 2021 on Coordination of Implementation Spatial planning
