UU no. 28 of 2002 concerning Building Construction

Chapter 7: Guidance


Article LAW NOTES

42

(1) The government organizes national building development to improve compliance with requirements and orderly building management.

(2) The Regional Government shall carry out guidance on the management of building construction as referred to in paragraph (1) in the region.

(3) Some of the organization and implementation of guidance as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) is carried out together with the community related to building construction.

(4) The Regional Government and the community in carrying out the development as referred to in paragraph (2) and paragraph (3) shall empower communities that are not yet able to fulfill the requirements as referred to in Chapter IV.

(5) Provisions regarding the development of building structures as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), paragraph (3), and paragraph (4) are regulated further.
