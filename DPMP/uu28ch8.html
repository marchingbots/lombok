UU no. 28 of 2002 concerning Building Construction

Chapter 8: Sanctions


Article LAW NOTES

44

Every owner and/or user who does not fulfill the obligation to fulfill the function, and/ or requirements, and/or management of the building as referred to in this law will be subject to administrative sanctions and/or criminal sanctions.


45

(1) Administrative sanctions as referred to in Article 44 may be in the form of:

a. written warning,

b. limitation of construction activities,

c. temporary or permanent suspension of construction work,

d. temporary or permanent suspension of building use

e. freezing of building construction permit;

f. revocation of building construction permit;

g. freezing of building function certificate;

h. revocation of building function certificate; or

i. order to demolish a building.

(2) In addition to the imposition of administrative sanctions as referred to in paragraph (1), a fine of up to 10% (ten per hundred) of the value of the building being or having been constructed may be imposed.

(3) The type of sanctions imposed as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) is determined by the severity of the violation committed.

(4) Provisions regarding the procedures for imposing sanctions as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), and paragraph (3) are further regulated by Government Regulation.


46

(1) Every owner and/or user of a building who does not comply with the provisions of this law shall be subject to a maximum prison sentence of 3 (three) years and/or a maximum fine of 10% (ten per hundred) of the value of the building, if this results in loss of property of another person.

(2) Every owner and/or user of a building who does not does not comply with the provisions of this law, shall be subject to a maximum prison sentence of 4 (four) years and/or a maximum fine of 15% (fifteen percent) of the value of the building, if it results in an accident to another person resulting in permanent disability.

(3) Every owner and/or user of a building who does not comply with the provisions of this law shall be subject to a maximum prison sentence of 5 (five) years and/or a maximum fine of 20% (twenty percent) of the value of the building, if this results in the loss of life of another person.

(4) In the judicial process for actions as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2) and paragraph (3), the judge shall take into account the considerations of the building expert team.

(5) Provisions regarding the procedures for imposing sanctions as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), and paragraph (3) are further regulated by Government Regulation.


47

(1) Any person or body who, due to negligence, violates the provisions stipulated in this law, resulting in a building being unfit for function, may be subject to imprisonment and/or a fine.

(2) The imprisonment and/or fine as referred to in paragraph (1) includes:

a. imprisonment for a maximum of 1 (one) year and/or a fine of a maximum of 1% (one per hundred) of the value of the building if it results in loss of another person's property;

b. imprisonment for a maximum of 2 (two) years and/or a fine of a maximum of 2% (two per hundred) of the value of the building if it results in an accident for another person resulting in a lifelong disability.

c. imprisonment for a maximum of 3 (three) years and/or a fine of a maximum of 3% (three per hundred) of the value of the building if it results in the loss of another person's life.

(3) Provisions regarding the procedures for imposing sanctions as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) are further regulated by Government Regulation.
