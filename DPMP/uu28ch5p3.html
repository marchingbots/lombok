UU no. 28 of 2002 concerning Building Construction

Chapter 5: BUILDING CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT

Part 3: Utilization


Article LAW NOTES

37

(1) Utilization of a building is carried out by the owner or user of the building after the building has been declared to meet the functional suitability requirements.

(2) A building is declared to meet the functional suitability requirements if it meets the technical requirements, as referred to in Chapter IV of this law.

(3) Regular maintenance, care and inspection of buildings must be carried out to ensure they continue to meet functional suitability requirements.

(4) In utilizing a building, the owner or user of the building has the rights and obligations as regulated in this law.

(5) Provisions regarding procedures for maintenance, care and periodic inspection of buildings as referred to in paragraph (3) are further regulated by Government Regulation.
