UU no. 28 of 2002 concerning Building Construction

Chapter 5: BUILDING CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT

Part 5: Demolition


Article LAW NOTES

39

(1) Buildings can be demolished if:

a. not functional and cannot be repaired;

b. can cause danger in the use of the building and/or its environment;

c. does not have a building permit.

(2) Buildings that can be demolished as referred to in paragraph (1) letters a and b are determined by the Regional Government based on the results of a technical assessment.

(3) Technical assessments of building structures as referred to in paragraph (2), except for residential houses, are carried out by technical assessors and their procurement is the responsibility of the building owner.

(4) Demolition of buildings that have a broad impact on public safety and the environment must be carried out based on a technical demolition plan that has been approved by the Regional Government.

(5) Provisions regarding procedures for demolishing buildings as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), paragraph (3), and paragraph (4) are further regulated by Government Regulation.
