UU no. 28 of 2002 concerning Building Construction

Chapter 1


Article LAW NOTES

Menimbang

a. that national development aims to create a just and prosperous society that is materially and spiritually equitable based on Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution;

b. that buildings are important as places for humans to carry out their activities to achieve various targets that support the realization of national development goals;

c. that the building must be organized in accordance with letter b, realized in accordance with its function, and the administrative and technical requirements for the building must be met;

d. that in order for building construction to be carried out in an orderly manner and realized in accordance with its function, the role of the community and development efforts are required;

e. that based on the considerations as referred to in letters a, b, c and d above, it is necessary to establish a Law on Buildings;
What is role of community?

1

A building is a physical form resulting from construction work that is integrated with its location, partly or wholly located above and/or in land and/or water, which functions as a place for humans to carry out their activities, whether for housing or residence, religious activities, business activities, social activities, cultural activities, or special activities.
partly or wholly located above and/or in land

2

The implementation of building construction is a development activity that includes the technical planning process and construction implementation, as well as utilization, preservation and demolition activities.
technical planning
construction implementation
other activities

3

Utilization of building structures is the activity of utilizing building structures in accordance with their designated functions, including maintenance, care and periodic inspection activities.
designated functions
maintenance
periodic inspection

4

Maintenance is an activity to maintain the reliability of a building and its infrastructure and facilities so that it remains functional.

5

Maintenance is the activity of repairing and/or replacing parts of a building, components,building materials, and/or infrastructure and facilities so that the building remains functional.

6
Periodic Inspections are activities to check the reliability of all or part of a building, its components, building materials, and/or infrastructure and facilities within a certain time period in order to declare the suitability of the building's function.
what is the time period?

7
Preservation is the activity of caring for, restoring and maintaining buildings and their environment to restore the reliability of the building to its original state or to the condition according to the desired period.

8
Demolition is the activity of dismantling or demolishing all or part of a building, components, building materials, and/or infrastructure and facilities.

9
The Owner of a building is a person, legal entity, group of people or association, who according to law is legally the owner of the building.

10
Building Users are building owners and/or non-building owners based on an agreement with the building owner, who use and/or manage the building or part of the building in accordance with the designated function.
includes guests?

11
A Technical Assessor is an individual or legal entity who has a certificate of expertise to carry out technical assessments of the functional suitability of a building in accordance with applicable statutory provisions.

12
The Community is an individual, group, legal entity or business, and institution or organization whose activities are in the field of building construction, including customary law communities and expert communities, who have an interest in the organization of building construction.

13
Building infrastructure and facilities are complete facilities inside and outside the building that support the fulfillment of the building's function.

14
The Central Government hereinafter referred to as the Government, is the apparatus of the Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia which consists of the President and ministers.

15
The Regional Government is the head of the district or city along with other autonomous regional apparatus as the regional executive body, except for the Special Capital Region of Jakarta Province, which is the governor.
