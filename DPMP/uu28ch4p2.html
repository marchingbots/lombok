UU no. 28 of 2002 concerning Building Construction

Chapter 4: BUILDING REQUIREMENTS

Part 2: Administrative Requirements for Building Construction


Article LAW NOTES

8
(1) Every building must meet administrative requirements which include:

a. land rights status and/or utilization permit from the land rights holder;

b. building ownership status; and

c.building construction permit in accordance with the provisions of regulations.

(2) Every person or legal entity may own a building or part of a building.

(3) The Regional Government is obliged to record building records for the purposes of orderly development and utilization.

(4) Provisions regarding building permits, ownership and building data collection as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2) and paragraph (3) are further regulated by Government Regulation.
