Article LAW NOTES

9 - General
(1) The building layout requirements as referred to in Article 7 paragraph (3) include requirements for the designation and intensity of the building, building architecture, and environmental impact control requirements.

(2) The building layout requirements as referred to in paragraph (1) are further stipulated in the building and environmental layout plan by the Regional Government.

(3) Provisions regarding the procedures for preparing building and environmental plans as referred to in paragraph (2) are further regulated by Government Regulation.

10 - Allocation Requirements and Building Intensity

(1) The requirements for the designation and intensity of building construction as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) include requirements for the designation of the location, density, height and building clearance determined for the location in question.

(2) The Regional Government is obliged to provide and give open information about the requirements for the designation and intensity of building construction for the community who need it.

(1) The requirements for location allocation as referred to in Article 10 paragraph (1) are implemented based on provisions regarding spatial planning.

(2) Buildings constructed above and/or below ground, water and/or public infrastructure and facilities must not disrupt environmental balance, the protective function of the area and/or the function of the public infrastructure and facilities concerned.

(3) Provisions regarding the construction of buildings as referred to in paragraph (2) are further regulated by Government Regulation.

12

(1) The building density and height requirements as referred to in Article 10 paragraph (1) include the building base coefficient, building floor coefficient and building height in accordance with the provisions stipulated for the location in question.

(2) Requirements for the maximum number of floors in a building or part of a building constructed below ground level must take into account the required safety, health and environmental carrying capacity.

(3) Building structures must not exceed the maximum density and height provisions set for the location in question.

(4) Provisions regarding the procedures for calculating and determining density and height as referred to in paragraph (3) are further regulated by Government Regulation.

(1) The requirements for building clearance as referred to in Article 10 paragraph (1) include:

a. the boundary line of the building with the road axis, river bank, beach edge, railway line, and/or high voltage network;

b. the distance between the building and the plot boundaries, and the distance between the road axis and the yard fence permitted at the location in question. .

(2) Requirements for the clearance of buildings or parts of buildings constructed below ground level must take into account location boundaries, security and not interfere with utility functions and the implementation of construction.

(3) Provisions regarding building clearance requirements as referred to in paragraph (2) are further regulated by Government Regulation.

14 - Building Architectural Requirements
(1) The architectural requirements for buildings as referred to in Article 9 paragraph (1) include requirements for the appearance of the building, interior layout, balance, harmony and compatibility of the building with its environment, as well as consideration of the balance between local socio-cultural values and the application of various architectural and engineering developments.

(2) The requirements for the appearance of buildings as referred to in paragraph (1) must take into account the form and characteristics of the architecture and the surrounding environment.

(3) The spatial planning requirements in buildings as referred to in paragraph (1) must take into account the function of the space, the architecture of the building and the reliability of the building.

(4) The requirements for balance, harmony and compatibility of a building with its environment as referred to in paragraph (1) must take into account the creation of space outside the building, green open space that is balanced, harmonious and in harmony with its environment.

(5) Provisions regarding the appearance of buildings, interior layout, balance and harmony of buildings with their environment as referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (2), paragraph (3) and paragraph (4) are further regulated by Government Regulation.

15 - Environmental Impact Control Requirements

(1) The application of environmental impact control requirements only applies to buildings that can have a significant impact on the environment.

(2) The requirements for controlling environmental impacts on buildings as referred to in paragraph (1) are in accordance with the provisions of applicable laws and regulations.
