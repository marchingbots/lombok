UU no. 28 of 2002 concerning Building Construction

Chapter 5: BUILDING CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT

Part 2: Development


Article LAW NOTES

35

(1) Building construction is carried out through planning and implementation stages along with supervision.

(2) Construction of buildings can be carried out either on land owned by oneself or on land owned by another party.

(3) Construction of a building on land belonging to another party as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out based on a written agreement between the land owner and the building owner.

(4) Construction of a building may be carried out after the technical plan for the building has been approved by the Regional Government in the form of a building permit, except for special function buildings.

36

(1) Approval of technical plans for buildings for public interest is determined by the Regional Government after receiving technical considerations from a team of experts.

(2) Approval of the technical plan for a special function building is determined by the government after receiving technical considerations from a team of experts.

(3) The membership of the building expert team as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) is ad hoc and consists of experts required according to the complexity of the building.

(4) Provisions regarding the procedures for approving technical plans for building structures as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) and the membership of the building expert team as referred to in paragraph (3) are further regulated by Government Regulation.
