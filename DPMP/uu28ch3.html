UU no. 28 of 2002 concerning Building Construction

Chapter 3: Building Functions


Article LAW NOTES

2

(1) The functions of a building include residential, religious, business, social and cultural functions, as well as special functions.

(2) Residential buildings as referred to in paragraph (1) include buildings for single-family homes, terraced houses, flats and temporary housing.

(3) Buildings for religious purposes as referred to in paragraph (1) include mosques, churches, temples, monasteries and Chinese temples.

(4) Business function buildings as referred to in paragraph (1) include buildings for offices, trade, industry, hotels, tourism and recreation, terminals and storage.

3

The purpose of building regulations is to:

1. create functional buildings that comply with building regulations that are harmonious and in harmony with their environment;

2. create orderly building management that guarantees the technical reliability of buildings in terms of safety, health, comfort and convenience;

3. create legal certainty in building management.
