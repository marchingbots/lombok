UU no. 28 of 2002 concerning Building Construction

Chapter 6: Role of Society


Article LAW NOTES

42

(1) The role of the community in the management of building construction can:

a. monitor and maintain order in management;

b. provide input to the Government and/or Regional Government in improving regulations, guidelines and technical standards in the field of building construction;

c. submitting opinions and considerations to the authorized agency regarding the preparation of building and environmental planning plans, technical plans for certain building structures, and implementation activities that have a significant impact on the environment;

d. carrying out class action lawsuits against buildings that disturb, harm, and/or endanger the public interest.

(2) Provisions regarding the role of the community as referred to in paragraph (1) are further regulated by Government Regulation.
