UU no. 28 of 2002 concerning Building Construction

Chapter 5: BUILDING CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT

Part 6: Rights and Obligations of Building Owners and Users.


Article LAW NOTES

40

(1) In the management of building construction, building owners have the right:

a. to obtain approval from the Regional Government for the technical plan for the building that has fulfilled the requirements;

b. to carry out the construction of the building in accordance with the permits that have been determined by the Regional Government;

c. to obtain a letter of determination for the building and/or environment that is protected and preserved from the Regional Government;

d. to obtain in accordance with statutory regulations from the Regional Government because the building is determined as a building that must be protected and preserved;

e. change the function of a building after obtaining written permission from the Regional Government;

f. receive compensation in accordance with statutory regulations if the building is demolished by the Regional Government or another party through no fault of his/her own.

(2) In the management of building construction, the building owner has the following obligations:

a. to provide a technical plan for the building which meets the requirements set out in accordance with its function;

b. have a building permit (IMB);

c. carry out the construction of the building in accordance with the approved technical plan and be carried out within the validity period of the building permit;

d. request approval from the Regional Government for changes to the technical plans for the building that occur during the construction implementation stage.


41

(1) In the management of building construction, owners and users has the right:

a. to know the procedures/processes for organizing building construction

b. to obtain information about the location designation and building intensity at the location and/or space where the building will be built;

c. obtain information about the requirements for building reliability;

d. obtain information about building regulations which is fit for function;

e. obtain information about buildings and/or environments that must be protected and preserved.

(2) In the management of building construction, building owners and users have the following obligations:

a. to utilize the building in accordance with its function;

b. to maintain and/ or care for the building periodically;

c. to complete guidelines/ instructions for the implementation of the use and maintenance of the building;

d. to carry out periodic inspections of the functional suitability of the building.

e. to repair buildings that have been determined to be unsuitable;

f. demolishing a building that has been determined to be unfit for use and cannot be repaired, may cause danger in its use, or does not have a building permit without disturbing safety and order.
