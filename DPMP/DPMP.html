- licensing requirements for foreign investment in Indonesia
1. Register your business on the OSS website, by preparing a notarial deed, AHU from the Ministry of Law and Human Rights, Taxpayer Identification Number (NPWP)
2. Manage spatial planning compliance online via the OSS website for buildings that will be assessed by the central investment ministry.
3. Managing environmental permits in the form of an environmental impact analysis which will be assessed by a team from the AMDAL net system which is integrated with OSS

Register for a building construction certificate from the Regional Spatial Planning Agency via the SIMBG application along with processing business permits.

for general spatial planning the required conditions Drawing polygon maps, floor plans of the space to be built, land certificates, taxpayer numbers, building drawings
