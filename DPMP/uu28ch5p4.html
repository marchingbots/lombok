UU no. 28 of 2002 concerning Building Construction

Chapter 5: BUILDING CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT

Part 4: Preservation


Article LAW NOTES

38

(1) Buildings and their surroundings which are designated as cultural heritage in accordance with statutory regulations must be protected and preserved.

(2) The determination of buildings and their surroundings which are protected and preserved as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Regional Government and/or the Government by taking into account statutory provisions.

(3) The implementation of repairs, restoration, protection and maintenance of buildings and their surroundings as referred to in paragraph (1) may only be carried out as long as it does not change the value and/or character of the cultural heritage they contain.

(4) Repairs, restoration and utilization of cultural heritage buildings and environments that are carried out in violation of the provisions on the function and/or character of cultural heritage must be returned in accordance with statutory regulations.

(5) Provisions regarding protection and preservation as referred to in paragraph (1) and paragraph (2) and the technical implementation of repairs, restoration and utilization as referred to in paragraph (3) and paragraph (4) are further regulated by Government Regulation.
