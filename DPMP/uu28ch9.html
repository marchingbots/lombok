UU no. 28 of 2002 concerning Building Construction

Chapter 9: Transitional Provisions


Article LAW NOTES

48

(1) Existing laws and regulations regarding building construction which do not conflict with this law shall remain in effect until new implementing regulations are issued based on this law.

(2) Buildings that have obtained permits issued by the Regional Government before this law comes into effect are declared to still be valid.

(3) Buildings that have been erected but do not yet have a building permit at the time this law comes into effect must obtain a certificate of functional suitability based on the provisions of this law to obtain a building permit.
