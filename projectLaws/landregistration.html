3. PATOK INSTALLATION

The applicant shall install boundary markers after obtaining the consent of the adjacent landowner.

The installation and maintenance of boundary markers as referred to in paragraph 19A(1) are the responsibility of the applicant.
REGULATION:
PM 3/1997 Pasal 19A(1) and 19A(3)

After obtaining the consent of the adjacent landowner.

REGULATION:
PM 3/1997 Pasal 19A(1)

Boundary determination is carried out by a surveyor based on the Statement of Boundary Mark Installation and Approval of the Adjacent Owner.

Boundary markers are installed at each corner of the land boundary and, if deemed necessary by the officer conducting the measurement, also at specific points along the boundary line of the land parcel.

For boundary corners that are clearly located because they are marked by permanently installed objects such as concrete fences, walls, or wire fence posts/reinforcing stakes, boundary markers do not need to be installed.

To install boundary markers as referred to in paragraph (1), photographs of the installed boundary markers shall be taken, accompanied by information about the location, coordinates, or geotagging.
REGULATION:
PM 3/1997 Pasal 19A(2), 19B(1), 21(1) and (2)

The installation of boundary markers as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be outlined in a Statement of Boundary Mark Installation and Approval of the Adjacent Landowner.

The photographic results of boundary markers as referred to in paragraph (2) and the Statement of Boundary Mark Installation and Approval of the Adjacent Landowner as referred to in paragraph (4) shall be required as part of the application.

The Statement of Boundary Mark Installation and Approval of the Adjacent Landowner shall be prepared in accordance with the format set out in Attachment I, which is an integral part of this Ministerial Regulation.
REGULATION:
PM 3/1997 Pasal 19A(4), (5), and (6)


4. BOUNDARY DETERMINATION

Boundary determination is carried out by a surveyor based on the Statement of Boundary Mark Installation and Approval of the Adjacent Owner.
REGULATION:
PM 3/1997 Pasal 19B(1)

Boundary determination is carried out at the location of the land parcel to be measured.
REGULATION:
PM 3/1997 Pasal 19B(2)

a. the surveyor reads the Statement of Boundary Mark Installation and Approval of the Adjacent Owner in the presence of the applicant or Interested Party; and

b. the applicant indicates the boundary of the land parcel being requested.
If boundary determination is carried out simultaneously with boundary delineation, the results of the boundary delineation are recorded in a Boundary Delineation Report (form 201A), which is approved by the relevant Rights Holder and the adjacent owner.
REGULATION:
PM 3/1997 Pasal 19B(2)

The installation of boundary markers as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be outlined in a Statement of Boundary Mark Installation and Approval of the Adjacent Landowner.

The photographic results of boundary markers as referred to in paragraph (2) and the Statement of Boundary Mark Installation and Approval of the Adjacent Landowner as referred to in paragraph (4) shall be required as part of the application.

The Statement of Boundary Mark Installation and Approval of the Adjacent Landowner shall be prepared in accordance with the format set out in Attachment I, which is an integral part of this Ministerial Regulation.
REGULATION:
PM 3/1997 Pasal 19A(4), (5), and (6)

Boundary designation and boundary determination activities may utilize information and communication technology that takes into account cross-aspects of space and time, as well as interactive digital communication, such as Augmented Reality/Virtual Reality (AR/VR), video call/audiovisual communication applications, or other technologies.

The results of boundary designation and boundary determination activities as referred to in paragraph 19C(1) shall be in the form of an Electronic Document recording.
REGULATION:
PM 3/1997 Pasal 19C(1) and (2)
