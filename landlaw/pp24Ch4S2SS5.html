Peraturan Pemerintah (PP) No. 24 Tahun 1997 tentang Pendaftaran Tanah


Government Regulation (PP) No. 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration



Chapter 4: FIRST-TIME LAND REGISTRATION.
Section 2: Collection and Processing of Physical Data.
Subsection 5: Making of Daftar Tanah (Land Registers)


Article Description Notes
21(1)
Land parcels which have been mapped or whose registration numbers have been added to the relevant cadastral maps shall be recorded in daftar tanah (land registers).
21(2)
The format and content of land registers and how to fill in, store and maintain land registers shall be determined by the Minister.


Other Notes:

None.
