1. Undang-Undang No. 5 Tahun 1960 tentang Peraturan Dasar Pokok-Pokok Agraria (UUPA)


Law no. 5 of 1960 concerning Basic Regulations on Agrarian Principles (UUPA)


NOTES


Chapter 2: The Rights on Land, Water, and Air Space, and Land Registration.
The Right of Using Air Space:


Article 48(1): The right of using air space authorizes the utilization of energy and elements in the air space for the purposes of maintaining the development of fertility of the earth, water and natural resources contained therein and other matters relating thereto.

Article 48(2): The right of air space shall be regulated by government regulation.

None.
