Peraturan Pemerintah (PP) No. 24 Tahun 1997 tentang Pendaftaran Tanah


Government Regulation (PP) No. 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration


NOTES


Chapter 5: MAINTENANCE OF LAND REGISTRATION DATA
Section 3: Storage of Public Registers and Documents.
Sub-section 6: Changes in Land Registration Data on the basis of Court Decision or Ruling.


Article Description Notes
55(1)
The Registrar of the Court shall notify the Head of the Land Office of the contents of all the decisions with permanent legal power made by the court and of all the decrees made by the Head of the Court which have caused changes in the data on a registered land parcel or an apartment for recording in the relevant buku tanah and, wherever possible, also in the relevant certificate and other registers.
55(2)
The recording as meant in paragraph (1) can also be carried out at the request of the interested party on the basis of an official copy of the decision of the Court which has permanent legal power or a copy of the decree of the Head of the Court that the said party has submitted to the Head of the Land Office.
55(3)
The recording of the nullification of a land right, Hak Pengelolaan (Right of Management), and apartment ownership right on the basis of the decision of the Court shall be carried out after a decree concerning the nullification of the right in question has been obtained from the Minister or from the Official appointed by the Minister as meant in Article 52(2).


Other Notes:

None.
