1. Undang-Undang No. 5 Tahun 1960 tentang Peraturan Dasar Pokok-Pokok Agraria (UUPA)


Law no. 5 of 1960 concerning Basic Regulations on Agrarian Principles (UUPA)


NOTES


Chapter 1: Basic Principles and Provisions


Article 2(3): The state's authority of control is exercised in order to achieve the maximum prosperity of the people in the sense of happiness, welfare and freedom in society and the constitutional state of Indonesia which is independent, sovereign, just and prosperous.

Article 14(1): the state shall draw up a general plan concerning reservation, appropriation and use of the earth (d) for needs of developing industries, transmigration and mining.

Article 14(2): Regional Govt shall regulate the reservation, appropriation, and use of earth water and air space for their regions in line with conditions in the respective regions.

Article 14(3): Regional Govt Regulations shall come into force after having been sanctioned as for First Level Regions by the Presidential, for the Second Level Region by Governor/Head of Region concerned; and the Third Level Region by the Bupati/Walikota of the Region concerned.

Article 15: The cultivation of land, including the increase of its fertility and the prevention of its damage, is the duty of every person corporation having legal relations with mentioned land, with due consideration to the economically weak party.


Other Notes:

Gives overall land use rights similar to nz - happiness, wealth creation, etc.
Article 15 on preservation of land via the prevention of damage is key.
