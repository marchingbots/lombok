Regulation of the Minister of Agrarian Affairs and Spatial Planning/Head of the National Land Agency Number 5 of 2025


Delegation of Authority to Determine Land Rights and Land Registration Activities


ENG translation
Legal Synopsis


Article Description Notes
Menimbang / Considering
Delegation of Authority to Determine Land Rights and Land Registration Activities
2(2)
Basis of delegation inclues (d) land value; and (e) potential risk of dispute or conflict.
2(3)
Criteria in (2) are basis of classifying the determination of regional categories in the context of delegating authority to determine land use rights.
Business use rights = category 1 -3; high, moderate and low value indicator.
Building use rights = category 1-4; very high, high, moderate, and low value indicator.
Pacitan is Category 3 > East Java - All Districts not included in category 1 or 2.
2(4)
Delegation of authority refered to in (2) includes the following activities:
(a)determination of land rights including provision of recommendation for re-determination of land rights;
(b) land registration including
(1.) land and space surveys, measurements, and mapping
(2.) signing Land Plot Map, Spatial Map, Measurement Letter.
(3.) signing of Land Books and Certificates, and/or ratification of services results.
4(1)
Authority to determine land rights may be PARTIALLY DELEGATED to head of Regional Office or head of the Land Office through delegation of authority.
5(1)
The Head of the Regional Office makes decisions regarding:
(b) ownership rights for individuals of non-agricultural land with an area of more than 5000m2 up to 15,000m2.
7(1)
The head of Regional Office makes decisions regarding:
(a) buidling use rights for individuals on state land with an area of more than 10,000m2 up to 20,000m2
(b) building use rights for legal entities on state land with the following provisions:
1. category 1 for areas up to 30,000m2
10(1)
The Head of the Land Office makes decisions regarding:
(b) ownership rights for individuals over non-agricultural land with an area of no more than 5000m2
12(1)
The Head of the Land Office makes decisions regarding:
(a) building use rights for individuals on state land with an area up to 10,000m2
(b) buidling use rights for legal entities in the context of implementing housing construction for low-income communities in accordance with provisions of laws and regs; and
(c) extension/renewal of building use rights resulting from separation, division or merger land plots by/and still in the name of a housing management company with a land area resulting in separation, division or merger up to 600m2


14(1)
SPORADIC LAND MEASUREMENT is responsibility of Head of Land Office.

14(2)
Measurement of land with area up to 25 hectares is carried out by Land Office.
Measurement of land with area greater than 25 ha up to 1000 ha is carried out by Regional OFfice.
14(4)
The use of measurement results carried out by Regional Office and Ministry as referred to in (2b and c) shall be submitted to the Head of the Land Office.
14(5)
Submission of measurement applications as referred to in article 14(2) is addressed to authorized officials according to the scope of authority.
14(7)
Director General who has duties/functions in the field of surveying/mapping can sub-delegate the authority to measure land plots to the Head of the Regional Office or the Head of the Land Office "for specific reasons"
15(4)
Land Area Maps, Spatial Maps and Thematic Area Maps issued by the Regional Office are signed by the head of the division who has duties and functions in the field of surveying and mapping.
15(6)
Land Plot Maps, Spatial Maps, and Measurement Letters issued by Land Office are signed by the section head who has duties and functions in the field of surveying and mapping.
15(9)
Signing of Land Plot Map with Measurement Letter and Spatial Map with Measurement Letter which is the authority of the Land Office can be done at the same time.


Other Notes:

Land Plot Map : the result of mapping 1 or more plots of land on a sheet of paper with certain scale, the boundaries of which have been determined by an authorized official and are used for announcing physical data.

Spatial Map: a document resulting from measurements and mapping containing information on spatial objects that has been authorized by an authorized official and is used in land registration activities.

Thematic Area Maps : maps that contain information on control, ownership, use and utilization of land which can be supplemented with information, other regional themes within the requested area.

Measurement Letter : a document containing physical data on a plot of land in the form of a map and description.

Land Book : document in the form of a list containing legal data and physical data on a land registration object for which rights already exist.
