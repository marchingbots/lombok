1. Undang-Undang No. 5 Tahun 1960 tentang Peraturan Dasar Pokok-Pokok Agraria (UUPA)


Law no. 5 of 1960 concerning Basic Regulations on Agrarian Principles (UUPA)


NOTES


Chapter 2: The Rights on Land, Water, and Air Space, and Land Registration.
The Registration of Land:


Article 19(2): Registration mentioned in paragraph (1) of this Article covers:
(a) the measuring, mapping and recording the land;
(b) the registration of the rights on land transfer of these rights;
(c) the issue of certificates of rights on land, which will be valid as strong evident.

Article 19(4): Expenses related to registration ... shall be regulated by Government Regulations, with the provision that those who are without means shall be exempted from those expenses.
