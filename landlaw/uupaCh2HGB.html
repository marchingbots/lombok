1. Undang-Undang No. 5 Tahun 1960 tentang Peraturan Dasar Pokok-Pokok Agraria (UUPA)


Law no. 5 of 1960 concerning Basic Regulations on Agrarian Principles (UUPA)


NOTES


Chapter 2: The Rights on Land, Water, and Air Space, and Land Registration.
The Right of Building:


Article 35(1): The right of building is the right to build and to own buildings on land which is not one's property for a period of not longer than 30 years.

Article 35(2): At the request of the holder of the right and considering the necessity as well as the condition of the building, the period of time meant in paragraph (1) can be extended by a period of not longer than 20 years.

Article 35(3): The right of building may go over to and be transferred to another party.

Article 36(1): Those who can possess hak guna bangunang are (1)Indonesian citizen and (2) Corporation established according to Indonesian law and having their domicile in indoneisa.

Article 37: HGB is originated: (a)with regard to land which is directly controlled by the States because of the government verdict; (b) with regard to land under right property, because of an agreement authored nature between the owner of the land building, aiming at the establishment of the above-mentioned right.

Article 38(1): HGB including its requirements of granting, its transfer and annullment of that right must be registered according to provisions, mentioned in Article 19.

Article 38(2): The registration meant in paragraph (1) constitutes a strong evidence concerning the annulment of the right of building as well as the validity of the transfer above mentioned right, except in the case that those rights are annuled because of the expire of the period.

Article 39: HGB is annuled because: (a) its period expires; (b) its termination before the expiration of its period due to non-fulfilment of certain requirements; (c) its abandonment by the holder of the right before expiry of the period; (d) its annulment in the public interest; (e) of its lying fallow (being neglected); (f) destruction of land; (g) the provision of article 36(2)


Other Notes:

Check period of use. Be careful of neglect and destruction.
