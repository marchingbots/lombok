Peraturan Pemerintah (PP) No. 18 Tahun 2021


Government Regulation (PP) No. 18 of 2021 CONCERNING MANAGEMENT RIGHTS, LAND RIGHTS, STANDARD UNITS, AND LAND REGISTRATION



Chapter 7: Land Registration.
Part 5: Evidence of Old Rights


Article Section Description Notes
95 (1)
Written evidence of the former West Land rights is stated does not apply and its status becomes Land Directly controlled by the State.
95 (2)
Registration of former West Rights Land as referred to in paragraph (1) is based on a written statement of physical control, confirmed by two (2) witnesses and responsible for civil and criminal matters, which outlines:
a. The land truly belongs to the person concerned, not to anyone else, and its status is Land Directly Controlled by the State, not former customary land;
b. The land is physically controlled;
c. The control was carried out in good faith and openly by the person concerned as the person entitled to the land; and
d. The control is not disputed by any other party.
96 (1)(b)
Written evidence of former customary land owned by individuals must be registered within a maximum period of 5 (five) years from the enactment of this Government Regulation.
96 (2)
If the time period referred to in paragraph (1) expires, the written evidence of former customary land is declared invalid and cannot be used as proof of land rights and only serves as a guide for land registration.
97
Village certificates, and other similar certificates, which are intended as proof of land ownership and control, issued by the village head/sub-district head, may only be used as guidance for land registration.
98 (1)
Swapraja land or former swapraja land is land directly controlled by the state.
98 (2)
Swapraja or former swapraja land as referred to in paragraph (1) may be granted to former swapraja or former swapraja land holders, if they meet the requirements and manage or cultivate the land themselves for the benefit of the swapraja.
reading
98 (3)
Swapraja or former swapraja land as referred to in paragraph (1) that is controlled by another party, is given to the party who cultivates or works the land in good faith.
98 (4)
Concessions or leases of former Lrapus autonomous land are now under direct control of the state.
98 (5)
The provisions referred to in paragraph (1), paragraph (21), paragraph (3), and paragraph (4) do not apply to autonomous or former autonomous land regulated by law.
99
Further provisions regarding the implementation of electronic Land Registration, storage and presentation of electronic data and/or documents, the form, content, and procedures for electronically preparing Land Deed Official deeds, expedited Land Registration, electronic registration of mortgage rights, recording of sales and lease agreements, recording of disputed objects and EUO staff orders, changing building use rights and use rights to ownership rights, and the registration of former land rights or former customary land, as well as self-governing or former self-governing land, as referred to in Articles 84 to 98, are regulated in a Ministerial Regulation.


Other Notes:

None.
