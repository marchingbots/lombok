OVERVIEW

Electronic Land Information Services are the process of providing information electronically including confirmation of conformity of physical data and legal data, land title certificate, and other information at database.

Certificate Checking Service is a service for checking the conformity of physical data and legal data of the Land Title Certificate with electronic data on the database.

Buku Tanah is a document in the form of a list containing physical data and legal data of a land registration object for which a right already exists.

Physical Data is information regarding the location, boundaries, and land area and apartment units registered, including information regarding the existence of building or part of a building above it.

Legal Data is information regarding the legal status of registered land plots, apartment units, rights holders, and other parties as well as other burdens that burden the object.
