1. Undang-Undang No. 5 Tahun 1960 tentang Peraturan Dasar Pokok-Pokok Agraria (UUPA)


Law no. 5 of 1960 concerning Basic Regulations on Agrarian Principles (UUPA)


NOTES


Chapter 2: The Rights on Land, Water, and Air Space, and Land Registration.
The Right of Opening-Up Land of Collecting Forest Product:


Article 46(1): The right may only be possessed by Indonesian citizen.

Article 46(2): Right to collect forest products legally does not imply ownership with regard to land.


Other Notes:

This pertains to Aris the wood man.
