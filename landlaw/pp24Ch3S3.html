Peraturan Pemerintah (PP) No. 24 Tahun 1997 tentang Pendaftaran Tanah


Government Regulation (PP) No. 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration



Chapter 3: Fundamentals of Land Registration Administration.
Section Three: Territory of Land Registration Administration (Registry)


Article Section Description Notes
10 (1)
The territory of land registration administration is the village/kelurahan.
what does this mean?
10 (2)
In particular with regard to Hak Guna Usaha (Right to Cultivate), Hak Pengelolaan (Right of Management), Hak Tanggungan (Security Title), and Tanah Negara (State Land), the territory of their registration administration is the District/ Municipality.

None.
