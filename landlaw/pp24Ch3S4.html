Section Four: Implementation of Land Registration

12(1)
The activity of first-time land registration shall comprise the following:
(a)
collecting and processing physical data;
(b)
verifying rights and recording them;
(c)
issuing certificates;
(d)
presenting physical data and juridical data;
(e)
storing public registers and documents.
12(2)
The activity of maintaining land registration data shall comprise the following:
(a)
registering transfers and encumbrances;

registering other changes in land registration data
