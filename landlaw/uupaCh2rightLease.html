1. Undang-Undang No. 5 Tahun 1960 tentang Peraturan Dasar Pokok-Pokok Agraria (UUPA)


Law no. 5 of 1960 concerning Basic Regulations on Agrarian Principles (UUPA)


NOTES


Chapter 2: The Rights on Land, Water, and Air Space, and Land Registration.
The Right of Lease Land for Building:


Article 44(1): A person or corporation has right of lease land if he is entitled to utilize land owned by another for the purpose of building, by paying to its owner an amount of money as rent.

Article 44(2): The payment of rent may be effected: (a) Once or at intervals; (b) Before or after the use of land. Article 44(3): Agreements concerning lease of land may not be accompanied by conditions having elements of extortion.

Article 45: Those who may become holders of right to lease are: (a) Indonesian citizens; (b) foreigner residing in Indo; (c) corporation; (d) foreign corporation having a representative in Indo.


Other Notes:

Check period of use. Be careful of neglect and destruction.
