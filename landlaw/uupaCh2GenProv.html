1. Undang-Undang No. 5 Tahun 1960 tentang Peraturan Dasar Pokok-Pokok Agraria (UUPA)


Law no. 5 of 1960 concerning Basic Regulations on Agrarian Principles (UUPA)


NOTES


Chapter 2: The Rights on Land, Water, and Air Space, and Land Registration.
General Provisions:


Article 16(1): Rights on land include:
Hak Milik (right of Ownership),
Hak Guna Usaha (right of Exploitation),
Hak Guna Bangunan (right of Building),
Hak Pakai (right of use),
Hak Sewa (right of lease),
hak memungut hasil hutan (right of collecting forest Product),
other rights of temporary nature.

Article 16(2): The right of using water.

Article 18: In the public interest, including the interests of the Nation and State as well as the common interest of the people, the rights on land may be annulled, with due compensation and according to a procedure laid down by act.


Other Notes:

Gives different rights in land. Gives power to annull rights on land.
