Peraturan Pemerintah (PP) No. 24 Tahun 1997 tentang Pendaftaran Tanah


Government Regulation (PP) No. 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration


NOTES


Chapter 1: General Provisions.


Article # Term Definition
1 1 Land Registration
A series of activites conducted by government on an on-going basis and in an orderly manner which comprises the COLLECTION, PROCESSING, RECORDING, PRESENTATION, and MAINTENANCE of physical and juridical data in the form of maps and registers concerning land parcels on which rights have been established, and for apartment ownership rights as well as for the encumberance thereof.
1 2 Land Parcel
A part of the surface of the earth which is a parcel with boundaries.
1 3 State Land
Or land directly controlled by the State is land which is not possessed under a certain land right.
1 4 Hak Pengelolaan - Right of Management
The State's right to control, the authority to exercise which is partially delegated to the holder.
1 5 Land Rights
Rights as meant in Article 16 of Act No.5 of 1960 re: the Basic Provisions concerning the fundamentals of agrarian affairs, herein after referred to as UUPA.
1 6 Phyiscal Data
Information about the location, land boundaries, and area of a certain registered land parcel or apartment and about the struxtures or parts of structures standing thereon.
1 7 Juridical Data
Information about the legal status of a registered land parcel or apartment, about the right holder, about the rights of other parties on it, and about other encumberances thereon.
1 8 Adjudication
An activity within the context of the implementation of first-time land registration, which comprimises the collection and verification of physical data and juridical data concerning one land registration object or more for purposes related to its/their registration.
1 9 First-time Land Regstration
The activity of land registration which is conducted on land registration objects which have not been registered under government regulation No. 10 of 1961 re: Land Registration or under this govt regulations.
1 10 Systemic Land Registration
The activity of first-time land registration which is conducted simultaneously and covers all unregistered land-registration objects existing in all or part of the territory of a village/kelurahan.
1 11 Sporadic Land Registration
The activity of first-time land registration which is conducted on one land-registration object or more in the territoty of a village/kelurahan or in part thereof, either on an individual basis or on a mass basis.
1 12 Maintenance of Land Registration Data
The activity of land registration which aims at adjusting the physical data and juridical data contained in cadestral maps, daftar tanah (land registers), daftar nama(name registers), surat ukur (survey Documents), buku tanah (land books), and certificates to changes which take place subsequently.
1 13 Technical Base Point
A point whose coordinates are obtained from measurement and computation under a certain system and which functions as a control point or a reference point for boundary survey and reconstruction purposes.
a map that contains the distribution of technical base points within a specific area.(no. 7 of 2019)
1 14 Cadastral Base Map
A map which shows technical base points and geographic elements such as rivers, roads, structures, and the physical boundaries of the land parcels.
1 15 Cadastral Map
A map which depicts one land parcel or more for land recording purposes.
1 16 Daftar Tanah - Land Register
A document in the form of a list showing the identities of land parcels under a certain numbering system.
1 17 Surat Ukur - survey document
A document which contains the physical data on a land parcel in the form of a map and some description.
1 18 Daftar Nama - name register
A document in the form of a register which contains information about the possession of land under certain land right or under hak pengelolaan (the right of management) or information about the holding of an apartment ownership right by a certain individual or corporate body.
1 19 Buku Tanah
A document in the form of a register which contains the juridical data and physical data on a land register object on which a right has been established.
1 20 Certificate
A right-evidencing document as meant in Article 19(2)(c) of UUPA. It is issued for a land right, a hak pengelolaan, hak wakaf (right to edified land), an apartment ownership right, or a security title which has been recorded in the relevant buku tanah.
a document that proves rights as referred to in Article 19 paragraph (2) letter c of the UUPA for land rights, management rights, waqf land, ownership rights to apartment units, and mortgage rights, each of which has been recorded in the relevant land register.
(no.7 of 2019)
1 21 Minister
The Minister who is responsible for agrarian/land affairs.
1 22 Badan Pertanahan Nasional (BPN)
A non-departmental government institution whose duties are in the area of land.
1 23 Land Office
An operating unit of BPN at the district of municipality or at an administrative territory equivalent to a district or municipality, whose duties are to register land rights and to maintain public registers of land-registration data.
a vertical agency of the National Land Agency in a Regency/City that is directly under and responsible to the Minister through the Head of the BPN Regional Office.
(no.7 of 2019)
1 24 PPAT - land deed official
A public official authorized to make certain land deeds.
1 25 Gambar Ukur - Drawing Map
a document that displays a picture of one or more plots of land and its surrounding conditions, as well as data on the results of land measurements, including distances, angles, azimuths, or direction angles.
(no.7 of 2019)
1 26 Pengukuran Bidang Tanah Secara Sistematik - Systematic Land Surveying
the process of determining the boundaries of land plots located within one or more villages/sub-districts or parts of villages/sub-districts, or more, in order to implement systematic land registration.
(no.7 of 2019)
1 27 Pengukuran Bidang Tanah Secara Sporadik - Sporadic Land Surveying
the process of determining the boundaries of one or more land plots based on requests from rights holders or prospective new rights holders whose plots are located adjacent to each other or scattered within a village/sub-district, as part of sporadic land registration.
(no.7 of 2019)
1 28 Pemetaan Bidang Tanah - Land Mapping
the activity of depicting the results of land measurements systematically or sporadically using a specific method on a specific medium such as a sheet of paper, drafting film, or other media so that the location and size of the land area can be determined from the medium used to map the land area.
(no.7 of 2019)
1 29 Peta Bidang Tanah - Land Plot Map
the result of mapping one or more plots of land on a sheet of paper at a specific scale, the boundaries of which have been determined by an authorized official and are used for the announcement of physical data.
(no.7 of 2019)
1 30 Nomor Identifikasi Bidang Tanah (NIB) - Land Identification Number
a special identification number assigned to a land parcel that is unique to each land parcel throughout Indonesia.
(no.7 of 2019)
1 31 Pemetaan Bidang Tanah - Land Mapping

1 32 Order
the ranking of basic engineering points based on density and precision, so that they can be divided into 5 (five) ranks, namely order 0 to 4, and function as tie points..
(no.7 of 2019)
1 33 Pemegang Hak - Right Holder
individuals or legal entities that hold land rights, ownership rights to condominium units, or management rights, or nadzir (owner) in the case of waqf land, whether registered or unregistered.
(no.7 of 2019)
1 34 Kuasa - Proxy

(no.7 of 2019)
1 35 PPihak Yang Berkepentingan - Interested Parties

(no.7 of 2019)
1 36 Warkah - Deed

(no.7 of 2019)
