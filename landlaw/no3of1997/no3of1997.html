PM no. 3 of 1997

Peraturan Menteri / Ministerial Regulation 3 of 1997
CONCERNING IMPLEMENTING PROVISIONS FOR GOVERNMENT REGULATION NUMBER 24 OF 1997 CONCERNING LAND REGISTRATION


CHAPTER LAW NOTES

Menimbang / Meningat


Notes
Menimbang:
a. that in order to implement land registration as mandated by Law Number 5 of 1960 concerning Basic Agrarian Regulations, Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration has been issued as a replacement for Government Regulation Number 10 of 1961 concerning Land Registration;

b. that in this regard, it is necessary to establish further provisions to implement Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997 through a Regulation of the Minister of State for Agrarian Affairs/Head of the National Land Agency;
