Peraturan Menteri (PM) No. 3 Tahun 1997 tentang KETENTUAN PELAKSANAAN PERATURAN PEMERINTAH NOMOR 24 TAHUN 1997 TENTANG PENDAFTARAN TANAH


Government Regulation (PP) No. 24 of 1997 concerning implementing provisions for government regulation no 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration.


Chapter 2: Measurement and Mapping.
Part 5: Mapping Land Areas for Creating Registration Maps
Paragraph 1: Systematic Land Registration Activities (31 -34)

Article section Description Notes
31
(1) For the purposes of the announcement as referred to in Article 26 paragraph (1) of Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997, land plot maps shall be prepared.

(2) Land plot maps shall be prepared by plotting the results of land plot boundary measurements on a land plot map sheet, or by citing the boundaries of land plots that have been identified and delimited by the Adjudication Committee, if the available base map is a photographic map.

(3) Land plot maps as referred to in paragraph (2) shall be on 80 mg HVS paper, A3 size (double quarto) or other paper of a similar size.

(4) Land plot maps shall be signed by the Chair of the Adjudication Committee.

(5) Land plot maps as referred to in paragraph (1) shall contain the following information:

a. map title, namely "Land Plot Map";
b. neighborhood unit (RT/RW) number, name of sub-district/village, district/municipality, and province;
c. map scale;
d. north arrow;
e. land parcel boundaries;
f. roads, rivers, or other objects that can be used as location indicators;
g. land parcel identification number;
h. date and signature of the Chair of the Adjudication Committee.
32
(1) Mapping of land parcels for an area whose registration base map is a photographic map is carried out by extracting the boundaries of the land parcels from the photographic map, the boundaries of which have been identified and determined by the Adjudication Committee, and mapping them on the registration map sheet.

(2) If a registration base map in the form of a line map is available for an area, the measurements of the land parcels within that area are mapped on the registration base map.

(3) If the mapping of land parcels cannot be done directly on the base map for cartographic reasons, the mapping of the land parcels can be carried out on the registration map sheet, which is an extract of the registration base map.

(4) If the area or part of the village/sub-district designated as a systematic land registration location is not yet included in a registration base map, the mapping of the land plot shall be carried out simultaneously with the creation of the registration base map as referred to in Article 18.

(5) The registration map sheets referred to in paragraphs (1) and (3) shall be prepared in accordance with the following provisions:

a) the registration map shall be prepared on drafting film with the dimensions and format as referred to in Article 15 paragraph (2);
b) the division and numbering of the registration map sheets shall be in accordance with the provisions as referred to in Article 16;
c) each land plot shall be given a registration number;
d) the cartographic symbols used to create the registration map shall be prepared in accordance with the example listed in Attachment 8;
e) the name of the province shall be written in the upper left corner of the drawing area;
f) the name of the municipality/district shall be written in the upper center corner of the drawing area;
g) On the right side of the sheet, a legend box is provided for writing the map title, map scale, north direction, cartographic legend, map sheet location instructions, map creation information, village/sub-district and district names, and approval for use of the registration map;
h) The map sheet number is written on the top right side of the legend.


33
(1) If there is a rebuttal at the time of the announcement as referred to in Article 27 paragraph (1) of Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997 and based on the Adjudication Committee's investigation, there is an error regarding the land parcel measurements listed on the land parcel maps, then the land parcel maps and the mapping results on the base map or registration map sheets as referred to in Article 32 shall be amended.

(2) A new measurement drawing shall be made for the revised measurements of the land parcel or parcels as referred to in paragraph (1), and the measurements on the old measurement drawing shall be declared invalid.
34
(1) After the announcement as referred to in Article 31 paragraph (1) and the changes to the base map or land registration map sheet as referred to in Article 33 paragraph (1) are completed, the base map or registration map sheet will be approved for use as a registration map by the Chair of the Adjudication Committee.

(2) For areas where a registration map is already available as referred to in paragraph (1), land plot mapping will be carried out on the registration map.
