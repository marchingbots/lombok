Peraturan Menteri (PM) No. 3 Tahun 1997 tentang KETENTUAN PELAKSANAAN PERATURAN PEMERINTAH NOMOR 24 TAHUN 1997 TENTANG PENDAFTARAN TANAH


Government Regulation (PP) No. 24 of 1997 concerning implementing provisions for government regulation no 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration.


Chapter 2: Measurement and Mapping.
Part 4: Land Area Measurement (24 - 30)

Article section Description Notes
24
(1) The basic principles of land measurement must comply with the technical rules of measurement and mapping so that the land being measured can be mapped, its location and boundaries can be identified on the map, and its boundary points can be reconstructed in the field.

(2) Land measurement as referred to in paragraph (1) can be carried out using terrestrial, photogrammetric, satellite, or other methods.

(3) The basic principles of measuring aboveground and/or underground space must comply with the technical rules of 3-dimensional measurement and mapping.

25
(1) Land area measurements are in principle carried out using the national coordinate system.

(2) If the land area measurements referred to in paragraph (1) cannot be carried out using the national coordinate system, then the measurements are carried out using the local coordinate system.
26
(1) Land plot measurements in areas where a registration base map in the form of a photographic map or image is available are carried out by identifying the land plot whose boundaries have been determined in accordance with statutory provisions.

(2) Land plot boundaries identified on the photographic map or image can be measured in the field.

(3) Aboveground and underground space boundaries identified on the 3-dimensional map must be verified in the field.

(4) If boundary points cannot be identified on the photographic map or image due to vegetation or other visual obstructions, measurements are taken from adjacent boundary points or other points that can be identified on the photographic map or image, so that the boundary points can be marked on the photographic map or image by means of surface cutting.

(5) If the boundary points cannot be identified in the field due to location conditions such as swamps, peatlands, or other specific conditions that make it impossible to install boundary markers, photogrammetric measurements are carried out and the boundary points are determined by the Interested Party and agreed upon by the bordering parties on a photo map or image.
measuring officer determines boundary
27
(1) All land measurements are tied to the national technical base point, the nearest Continuously Operating Reference Station, and/or other details that are readily identifiable in the field and on the map.

(2) The binding as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out using the Network Transport of RTCM method via Internet Protocol or post-processing.
28
Measuring land parcels in land registration is carried out systematically Simultaneously with the creation of a base map, registration is carried out in accordance with the provisions in Article 18
29
(1) Sporadic land plot measurements in areas where a registration base map is not available but where a national technical base point is located less than 2 (two) kilometers from the land plot, must be tied to the national technical base point.

(2) For sporadic land plot measurements in areas where a registration base map and a national technical base point are not available, two or more local fourth-order technical base points must be created around the land plot to be measured, which serve as anchor points for the land plot measurements in the local coordinate system.

(3) Measurements of other land plots located on the same registration map sheet as the land plot referred to in paragraph (2) must be tied to the local technical base points referred to in paragraph (2).
30
(1) Each land plot measurement must be accompanied by a measurement drawing.

(2) The measurement drawing can depict one or more plots of land.

(3) The measurement drawing can be made on a checklist form, a photo map/line map, a blown-up aerial photograph, or other imagery.

(4) All data resulting from the land plot boundary measurements are recorded on the measurement drawing and must be used to restore the boundaries of the relevant plots of land if necessary.

(5) Each measurement drawing is assigned a measurement drawing number, sequentially numbered in checklist 302.

(6) Buildings on a plot of land are depicted on the measurement drawing.

(7) The measurement drawing includes the Land Plot Identification Number (NIB) and, if necessary, cartographic symbols.

30A
(1) The measurement drawings referred to in Article 30 may be prepared in the form of an electronic document.

(2) If the preparation of an electronic document is not yet possible, the measurement drawings referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) shall be prepared in the form of an analog document.

(3) The measurement drawings referred to in paragraph (1) shall contain at least:
a. boundary designation by the applicant/his/her attorney;
b. declaration of boundary determination by the measuring officer;
c. correction notes or quality control results by the designated official; and
d. inclusion of metadata such as the measurement equipment used, measurement methods, measurement data and results, boundary dispute resolution, and other technical data.

(4) In addition to depicting the land area, the Measurement Drawing also depicts buildings, buffer zones, water body boundaries such as river boundaries, coastal and road boundaries, conservation areas, relinquished land rights, or other social functions/public interests in accordance with the regional spatial plan for the location of the land area being measured.

(5) The Measurement Drawing is prepared in accordance with the format as set out in Attachment II, which is an integral part of this Ministerial Regulation.

30B
(1) Land measurement can utilize information and communication technology that takes into account cross-aspects of space and time, as well as interactive digital communication such as Augmented Reality/Virtual Reality (AR/VR) technology, video call/audiovisual communication applications, or other technologies.

(2) The measurement results as referred to in paragraph (1) are recorded in an Electronic Document.
