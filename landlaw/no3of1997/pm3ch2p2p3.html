Peraturan Menteri (PM) No. 3 Tahun 1997 tentang KETENTUAN PELAKSANAAN PERATURAN PEMERINTAH NOMOR 24 TAHUN 1997 TENTANG PENDAFTARAN TANAH


Government Regulation (PP) No. 24 of 1997 concerning implementing provisions for government regulation no 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration.


Chapter 2: Measurement and Mapping.
Part 2: Creating a Registration Base Map
Paragraph 2: Creating a Registration Base Map From Another Map (17)

Article section Description Notes
17
(1) The creation of a registration base map can also be carried out simultaneously with the measurement of the land parcel or parcels included therein.

(2) If the creation of a registration base map is carried out simultaneously with the measurement of the land parcel or parcels, the measurement of the land parcel shall be preceded by the measurement of a national fourth-order technical base point, which is linked to the nearest third-order or second-order national technical base points in the surrounding area.

(3) If there are no third-order or second-order national technical base points in the vicinity of the land in question, the creation of the registration base map must begin with the creation of technical base points using a local coordinate system, which, in the case of systematic land registration, must cover at least the area designated as the area for systematic land registration.

(4) If a fourth-order national technical base point becomes available in the area at a later date, the registration map in that area shall be transformed into a registration map using the national coordinate system.

(5) In the measurements carried out to create the basic registration map referred to in paragraph (1), in addition to the boundaries of the land plot, the situation/details surrounding it and, if necessary, the buildings on it are also included.
