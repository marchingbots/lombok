Peraturan Menteri (PM) No. 3 Tahun 1997 tentang KETENTUAN PELAKSANAAN PERATURAN PEMERINTAH NOMOR 24 TAHUN 1997 TENTANG PENDAFTARAN TANAH


Government Regulation (PP) No. 24 of 1997 concerning implementing provisions for government regulation no 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration.


Chapter 2: Measurement and Mapping.
Part 5: Mapping Land Areas for Creating Registration Maps
Paragraph 2: In Sporadic Land Registration Activities (35 - 39)

Article section Description Notes
35
(1) For the purposes of the announcement as referred to in Article 26 paragraph (1) of Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997, a map of the land plot or plots as referred to in Article 31 shall be prepared and signed by the Head of the Land Office or a designated official.

(2) If there is a dispute at the time of the announcement and, based on research by the authorized committee, an error is found regarding the measurements of the plots depicted, changes shall be made as referred to in Article 33.
35A
In determining the granting of Land Rights, the creation of Land Plot Maps resulting from measurements, which are under the authority of the Regional Office or Ministry, must be supplemented with thematic elements containing information in the following areas:

a. forest areas;
b. spatial patterns in accordance with the Spatial Plan;
c. areas where new permits for primary natural forests and peatlands have been discontinued;
d. conservation areas for water body boundaries or other conservation functions, including slopes exceeding 40% (forty percent);
e. permit areas in the forestry sector;
f. permit areas in the mining sector;
g. partnership plantation locations, in the case of Cultivation Rights (Hak Guna Usaha) applications;
h. other permit areas related to the utilization of natural resources; and/or
i. Thematic Area Maps containing land control, ownership, use, and utilization.


35B
(1) Changes and/or cancellations to Land Plot Maps may be made based on:

a. recommendations from the land inspection committee;
b. requests from landowners and/or objections from bordering parties, which will be followed up with boundary determination and re-measurement;
c. minutes of the review of technical studies and procedures for measuring and mapping the designated plot or other technical studies from the Head of the Land Office, Head of the Regional Office, the Director in charge of cadastral measurement and mapping, or the Director in charge of rights determination or a designated official; and
d. resolution of disputes over control and ownership of the land plot in question.

(2) Land Plot Maps remain valid as long as there are no physical changes to the land plot.
