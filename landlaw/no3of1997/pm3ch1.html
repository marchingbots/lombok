Peraturan Menteri (PM) No. 3 Tahun 1997 tentang KETENTUAN PELAKSANAAN PERATURAN PEMERINTAH NOMOR 24 TAHUN 1997 TENTANG PENDAFTARAN TANAH


Government Regulation (PP) No. 24 of 1997 concerning implementing provisions for government regulation no 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration.


Chapter 1: General Provisions.


Article # Term Definition
1 1 Technical Base Map
a map that displays the distribution of technical base points within a specific area.
1 2 Measurement Drawing
a document that includes a picture of one or more plots of land and its surrounding conditions, as well as data from land measurement results, including distances, angles, azimuths, or direction angles.
1 3 Systematic land surveying
the process of determining the boundaries of land plots located within one or more villages/sub-districts or parts of one or more villages/sub-districts in order to organize systematic land registration.
1 4 Sporadic land surveying
the process of determining the boundaries of one or more land plots based on requests from rights holders or prospective new rights holders whose plots are adjacent or scattered within a village/sub-district, as part of sporadic land registration.
1 5 Land mapping
the activity of depicting the results of land measurements systematically or sporadically using a specific method on a specific medium such as paper, drafting film, or other media so that the location and size of the land area can be determined from the medium where the land area is mapped.
1 6 Land Plot Map
the result of mapping one or more plots of land on a sheet of paper at a specific scale, the boundaries of which have been determined by an authorized official and are used for the announcement of physical data.
1 7 The Land Identification Number (NIB)
a special identification number assigned to each land parcel throughout Indonesia.
1 8 Order
the ranking of basic engineering points based on density and accuracy. Therefore, it can be divided into 5 (five) ranks, namely order 0 to 4, and functions as a tie point.
1 9 Rights Holder
a person or legal entity that holds land rights, ownership rights to condominium units, or management rights, or a nadzir in the case of waqf land, whether registered or unregistered.
1 10 Power of attorney
a person or legal entity who has received valid written power of attorney from rights holder.
1 11 Interested Parties
rights holders and other parties or parties have an interest in land.
1 12 Deed
a document that serves as proof of the physical and legal data of a land plot and is used as the basis for registering the land plot.
1 12A Certificate
A certificate is a document that proves rights as referred to in Article 19 paragraph (2) letter c of the UUPA for land rights, management rights, waqf land, ownership rights to apartment units, and mortgage rights, each of which has been recorded in the relevant land register.
1 12B Electronic System
An Electronic System is a series of electronic devices and procedures that function to prepare, collect, process, analyze, store, display, announce, transmit, and/or disseminate electronic information.
1 12C Electronic Signature
An Electronic Signature is a signature consisting of Electronic Information attached, associated, or linked to other Electronic Information used as a means of verification and authentication.
1 12D Deed
An Electronic Document is any electronic information created, forwarded, sent, received, or stored in analog, digital, electromagnetic, optical, or similar form, which can be viewed, displayed, and/or heard through a computer or electronic system, including, but not limited to, text, sound, images, maps, designs, photographs, or similar, letters, signs, numbers, access codes, symbols, or perforations that have meaning or significance or can be understood by a person capable of understanding them.
1 13 Minister
the Minister responsible for the agrarian/land sector.
The Minister of Agrarian Affairs and Spatial Planning/Head of the National Land Agency, hereinafter referred to as the Minister, is the Minister who administers government affairs in the agrarian/land and spatial planning sectors.
1 14 Regional Office
the Regional Office of the National Land Agency of the Province.
The Regional Office of the National Land Agency, hereinafter referred to as the Regional Office, is a vertical agency of the National Land Agency in the Province that is directly under and responsible to the Minister.
1 15 Land Office
the District/Municipal Land Office.
The Land Office is a vertical agency of the National Land Agency in a Regency/City that is directly under and responsible to the Minister through the Head of the BPN Regional Office.


Other Notes:

None.
