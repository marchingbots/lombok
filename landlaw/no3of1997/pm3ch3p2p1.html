Peraturan Menteri (PM) No. 3 Tahun 1997 tentang KETENTUAN PELAKSANAAN PERATURAN PEMERINTAH NOMOR 24 TAHUN 1997 TENTANG PENDAFTARAN TANAH


Government Regulation (PP) No. 24 of 1997 concerning implementing provisions for government regulation no 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration.


Chapter 3: First-time Land Registration.
Part 2: Sporadic Land Measurement
Paragraph 1: Sporadic Land Registration Application (73 - 76)

Article section Description Notes
73
(1) Sporadic land registration activities are carried out upon applications that are contained in a letter in the form as set out in Attachment 13.

(2) Applications as referred to in paragraph (1) include applications to:

a. measure land plots for specific purposes;

b. register new rights based on evidence as referred to in Article 23 of Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997;

c. register old rights as referred to in Article 24 of Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997.
evidence of new rights - article 23 pm no 24 of 1997
74
Applications for land measurement are submitted for:

a. preparation of new rights applications, such as the measurement of land parcels, small islands, and water areas;

b. division, separation, and merger of land parcels;

c. restoration of boundaries;

d. demarcation of boundaries for land consolidation;

e. inventory of land ownership and control in the context of land acquisition for development in the public interest in accordance with statutory provisions;

f. application for measurement of objects that are the subject of a court case and/or to implement a court decision; or

g. other purposes with the consent of the Rights Holder.


74A
(1) If land is the subject of a court case, measurements may be conducted at the request of the judge examining the case to determine the location and boundaries of the land being contested.

(2) The application for land measurement as referred to in paragraph (1) must be accompanied by:

a. an official copy of the letter from the court at the request of the judge examining the case; and
b. the applicant's identity.

(3) The costs of the measurement shall be borne by the plaintiff in accordance with statutory provisions.

(4) If the case referred to in paragraph (1) is:

a. a boundary dispute between adjacent parties, the boundary must be determined by the parties to the case; or
b. a dispute over ownership and control, the boundary must be determined by the plaintiff.

(5) To carry out the measurement as referred to in paragraph (1), the plaintiff must present the adjacent parties.

(6) The results of the land measurement as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of a Land Plot Map using a temporary identification number and shall be noted that the measurement was conducted for the purpose of a court application for the subject of the lawsuit being contested.

(7) If the subject of the lawsuit as referred to in paragraph (1) is land for which a Certificate has been issued in the name of someone other than the parties to the lawsuit or is an asset of a Government Agency/Regional Government/State-Owned Enterprise/Regional-Owned Enterprise, the judge handling the case shall present the Certificate owner or the user/manager of the land as an asset of the Government Agency/Regional Government/State-Owned Enterprise/Regional-Owned Enterprise.

(8) If the subject of the lawsuit as referred to in paragraph (7) has been subject to a court decision that has obtained permanent legal force, the temporary identification number that has been assigned cannot serve as the Land Plot Identification Number (NIB).

74B
(1) If the measurement is conducted in the context of implementing a court decision, the application must be submitted by the court clerk to determine the location and boundaries of the land.

(2) The application for land measurement as referred to in paragraph (1) must be accompanied by:

a. an official copy of the court decision that has obtained permanent legal force; and
b. the applicant's identity.

(3) The costs of the measurement as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be borne by the winner of the case in accordance with statutory provisions.

(4) The bailiff must determine the boundaries of the land according to the object of the lawsuit, and he or she is responsible for the location and boundaries of the land subject to execution that he or she indicates.

(5) If the measurement is carried out as referred to in paragraph (1), the winner of the case must present the adjacent parties.

(6) The results of the land area measurement as referred to in paragraph (1) shall be in the form of a Land Area Map using the Land Area Identification Number (NIB) and shall be noted that the measurement was carried out in the context of implementing the court decision.
75
The application as referred to in Article 73 paragraph (2) letter b must be accompanied by original documents to prove the rights to the land plot in question as referred to in Article 23 of Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997.


76
(1) The application referred to in Article 73 paragraph (2) letter c must be accompanied by original documents proving the existence of the relevant rights as referred to in Article 24 paragraph (1) of Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997, namely:

a. a gross deed of ownership rights issued based on the Oversight Ordinance (S.1834-27), which has been notated that the relevant ownership rights have been converted into ownership rights, or

b. a gross deed of ownership rights issued based on the Oversight Ordinance (S.1834-27) from the enactment of the UUPA until the date of land registration carried out according to Government Regulation Number 10 of 1961 in the relevant area; or

c. a certificate of ownership rights issued based on the relevant Swapraja Regulation, or

d. A land ownership certificate issued based on Ministerial Regulation Number 9 of 1959, or

e. a decree granting land ownership rights from an authorized official, either before or since the enactment of the UUPA, which does not require registration of the granted rights, but all obligations stated therein have been fulfilled, or

f. a Land Tax/Landrente certificate, girik, pipil, kekitir, and Verponding Indonesia before the enactment of Government Regulation Number 10 of 1961, or

g. a private deed of transfer of rights, witnessed by the Customary Head/Village/Kelurahan Head, made before the enactment of this Government Regulation, accompanied by the basis for the transferred rights, or

h. a deed of transfer of land rights made by a Land Deed Official (PPAT), for which the land has not been recorded, accompanied by the basis for the transferred rights, or

i. a waqf pledge deed/waqf pledge letter made before or since the implementation of Government Regulation Number 28 of 1977, accompanied by the basis of the rights being donated, or

j. an auction report prepared by an authorized Auction Official, for which the land has not been recorded, accompanied by the basis of the rights being transferred, or

k. a letter of appointment or purchase of a replacement land plot acquired by the Government or Regional Government, or

l. a land history certificate previously issued by the Land and Building Tax Office, accompanied by the basis of the rights being transferred, or

m. other forms of written evidence under any name as referred to in Articles II, VI, and VII of the Conversion Provisions of the UUPA.

(2) If proof of ownership of a plot of land as referred to in paragraph (1) is incomplete or non-existent, proof of ownership of that plot of land may be provided with other evidence, supplemented by a statement from the person concerned and reliable information from at least 2 (two) witnesses from the local community who are not related to the person concerned up to the second degree, either vertically or horizontally, who state that the person concerned is the true owner of the plot of land.

(3) If proof of land ownership as referred to in paragraphs (1) and (2) is missing, the application must be accompanied by:

a. a written statement from the applicant stating the following:
1) that the applicant has actually controlled the land in question for 20 consecutive years or more, or has obtained such control from another party or parties who have already controlled it, so that the period of control by the applicant and their predecessors totals 20 years or more;
2) that the land ownership has been exercised in good faith;
3) that the ownership has never been contested and is therefore considered recognized and confirmed by the customary law community or the village/sub-district concerned;
4) that the land is not currently in dispute;
5) that if the statement contains anything inconsistent with the facts, the signatory is prepared to be prosecuted before a judge, either criminally or civilly, for providing false information.

b. Statements from the Village Head/Sub-district Head and at least two (2) witnesses whose testimony is credible, due to their role as local traditional elders and/or long-time residents of the village/sub-district where the land in question is located and who are not related to the applicant to the second degree by either vertical or horizontal kinship, who confirm what the applicant states in the statement above, in the form set out in Attachment 14.


Appendix 14 in Article 76 paragraph (3) of the Regulation of the Minister of Agrarian Affairs/Head of the National Land Agency Number 3 of 1997 concerning Implementing Provisions of Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration, amended to the format of Appendix III as referred to in Article 76B paragraph (7)
76A
(1) Written evidence of former customary land owned by individuals in the form of a Land Tax Certificate (Petuk Pajak Bumi/Landrente), girik, pipil, kekitir, Indonesian Verponding, and other evidence of former customary land rights with other names or terms shall be declared invalid after 5 (five) years from the enactment of Government Regulation Number 18 of 2021 concerning Management Rights, Land Rights, Apartment Units, and Land Registration.

(2) If the time period referred to in paragraph (1) expires, then:

a. written evidence of former customary land as referred to in paragraph (1) cannot be used as evidence of Land Rights and only serves as guidance for land registration; and
b. the land status remains that of former customary land.

(3) Land registration as referred to in paragraph (2) is carried out through a rights recognition mechanism.

(4) The application for recognition of rights as referred to in paragraph (3) shall be accompanied by a written statement of physical control by the applicant and his/her civil and criminal responsibility, stating that:

a. the land truly belongs to the person concerned, not to anyone else, and that its status is former customary land, not State Land;
b. the land has been physically controlled for 20 (twenty) consecutive years or more;
c. the land was controlled in good faith and openly by the person concerned as the person entitled to the land;
d. there are no objections from other parties regarding the land owned and/or it is not in dispute;
e. there are no objections from creditors if the land is used as collateral for a debt; and
f. it is not an asset of the Government, Regional Government, or State-Owned Enterprise/Regional-Owned Enterprise and is not located in a Forest Area.

(5) The element of good faith as referred to in paragraph (4) letter c consists of the physical fact of continuously controlling, using, utilizing, and maintaining the land for a certain period of time and/or acquiring it in a manner that does not violate statutory provisions.

(6) The statement of physical control as referred to in paragraph (4) must be made with the following provisions:

a. witnessed by at least 2 (two) witnesses from the local community who are not related to the person concerned up to the second degree, either through vertical or horizontal kinship, who state that the person concerned is the true owner and person in control of the land; and
b. made based on truthful information and can be accounted for, either civilly or criminally, if any untruths are found in the statement at a later date.

(7) The Statement of Physical Control must be made in accordance with the format set out in Attachment III, which is an integral part of this Ministerial Regulation.
