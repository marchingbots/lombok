Peraturan Menteri (PM) No. 3 Tahun 1997 tentang KETENTUAN PELAKSANAAN PERATURAN PEMERINTAH NOMOR 24 TAHUN 1997 TENTANG PENDAFTARAN TANAH


Government Regulation (PP) No. 24 of 1997 concerning implementing provisions for government regulation no 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration.


Chapter 3: First-time Land Registration.
Part 2: Sporadic Land Measurement
Paragraph 2: Measurement (77-81)

Article section Description Notes
77
(1) Sporadic land measurement is essentially the responsibility of the Head of the Land Office.

a. To optimize the use of personnel and measurement equipment, and taking into account the technological capabilities of the surveying officers, the measurement of a land plot measuring between 10 and 1,000 hectares is carried out by the Regional Office;

b. The measurement of a land plot measuring more than 1,000 hectares is carried out by the National Land Agency, with the results submitted to the Head of the Land Office.

(2) Applications for measurement as referred to in paragraph (2) are submitted to the Head of the Land Office.

(3) Based on the appointment of the Deputy for Land Measurement and Registration, the measurement of large land plots or large plots may be carried out by a third party.

(4) The implementation of land plot measurements by third parties as referred to in paragraph (4) is supervised, and the results are validated by the Head of the Land Office, the Head of the Regional Office, or the Deputy for Land Measurement and Registration, in accordance with the authority referred to in paragraph (2).

(5) The implementation of land plot measurements by third parties as referred to in paragraph (4) is supervised, and the results are validated by the Head of the Land Office, the Head of the Regional Office, or the Deputy for Land Measurement and Registration, in accordance with the authority referred to in paragraph (2).
78
The duties of a surveyor are as follows:

a. prepare by creating a work map, conducting a review, risk analysis, and mitigation plan for the land to be measured, in order to address potential problems, including boundary disputes, overlapping, and other issues;

b. Coordination with relevant parties to minimize potential problems;

c. determining land boundaries based on a Statement of Boundary Mark Installation and Approval of the Bordering Parties;

d. d. assist in resolving disputes regarding boundaries plot of land as outlined in the Minutes Boundary Dispute Settlement (d.i. 200);

e. carrying out measurements and Field Mapping Land;

f. f. make measuring drawings and maps of land plots and/or minutes; And

g. Ensure that the measured area has been mapped on the registration map in the Computerized Land Activities Application, and, if necessary, arrange and correct the registration map.


79
After the measurement officer receives the measurement order, the following preparations are made:

a. planning measurements on a registration map or other eligible map and conducting a risk analysis and mitigation plan for the land area to be measured in relation to potential problems;

b. checking that the equipment complies with the location plan and that technical base points are available around the requested land plot; and

c. provide notification, either in writing or through other communication media, to the applicant regarding the time for implementing the boundary determination and measurement.



80
(1) Prior to conducting land measurements, a surveyor from the Land Office must first determine the boundaries of the land, and the applicant must install boundary markers in accordance with the provisions of Articles 19, 20, 21, 22, and 23.

(2) If the land boundary measurement is carried out by a third party, the determination of the land boundary is carried out by the Head of the Land Measurement and Registration Section or an officer appointed by him.

(3) Boundary determination is carried out after written notification is given to the measurement applicant and to the rights holders of the adjacent plots.

(4) The notification referred to in paragraph (3) must be given no later than 10 (ten) days before the boundary determination is carried out.

(5) Such notification must be made in a letter in the form listed in Attachment 15.
81
(1) After the boundary determination and installation of boundary markers are completed, the land plots are measured and mapped.

The measurements and mapping are carried out in accordance with the provisions in Chapter II, Parts Four and Five of these regulations.
