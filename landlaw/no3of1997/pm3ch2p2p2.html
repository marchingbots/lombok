Government Regulation (PP) No. 24 of 1997 concerning implementing provisions for government regulation no 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration.


Chapter 2: Measurement and Mapping.
Part 2: Creating a Registration Base Map
Paragraph 2: Creating a Registration Base Map From Another Map (17)

Article section Description Notes
17
(1) A registration base map may be created using another map that meets the following requirements:

a. the map has a scale of 1:1,000 or larger for urban areas, 1:2,500 or larger for agricultural areas, and 1:10,000 or smaller for large plantation areas;

b. the map referred to in letter a has a planimetric accuracy of greater than or equal to 0.3 mm on the map scale;

c. to determine the planimetric accuracy referred to in paragraph (1) letter b, the distances are checked at points that are easily identified in the field and on the map.

(2) If the map referred to in paragraph (1) is not in the national coordinate system, it must be transformed into the national coordinate system.
