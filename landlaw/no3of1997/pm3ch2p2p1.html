Government Regulation (PP) No. 24 of 1997 concerning implementing provisions for government regulation no 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration.


Chapter 2: Measurement and Mapping.
Part 2: Creating a Registration Base Map
Paragraph 1: Measurement and Mapping for Creating a Registration Base Map (12 -16)

Article section Description Notes
12
(1) Measurement and mapping for the creation of registration base maps are carried out using terrestrial, photogrammetric, or other methods.

(2) Terrestrial measurement and mapping are measurements and mapping carried out on the earth's surface.

(3) Photogrammetric measurement and mapping are measurements and mapping using aerial photography.

(4) Aerial photography is a photograph of the earth's surface taken from the air using a camera mounted on an aircraft and meeting the requirements.

13
(1) Registration base maps are created at a scale of 1:1,000 or larger for residential areas, 1:2,500 or larger for agricultural areas, and 1:10,000 for large plantation areas.

(2) Registration base maps can be line maps or photo maps.

(3) Registration base maps are created by linking them to national technical base points.

(4) Registration base maps that are still in the local coordinate system must be transformed into the national coordinate system.


14
The details measured in creating a registration base map include all or part of geographic elements such as rivers, roads, buildings, physical boundaries of land parcels, and elevations.
15
(1) Registration base maps in the form of line maps are made on drafting film, while registration base maps in the form of photo maps are made on bromide paper.

(2) Registration base maps or line maps are made with the following provisions:

a. The map face size is 50 cm x 50 cm and the drawing area size is 70 cm x 70 cm for a 1:1,000 scale map.
b. The map face size is 60 cm x 60 cm and the image area size is 80 cm x 80 cm for 1:2,500 scale map.
c. The size of the map face is 60 cm x 60 cm and the size of the image area is 60 cm x 60 cm for 1:10,000 scale maps.

(3) The registration base map in the form of a photo map is made with the following provisions:

a. size of map face and image area 50 cm x 50 cm for 1:1000 scale map;
b. The size of the map face and image area is 60 cm x 60 cm for a 1:2500 scale map and scale 1 : 10000;

(4) The cartographic symbols used to create the registration base map are made according to the example listed in Attachment 4.

(5) On the right side of the map sheet, space is provided for writing the title, map scale, north direction, map sheet location instructions, cartographic legend, map creation information, village/sub-district and district names, and the name of the third party responsible, if any.

(6) The name of the province is written in the upper left corner of the drawing area.

(7) The name of the city/district is written in the upper center of the drawing area.

(8) The registration base map number is written in the upper right corner of the legend.

16
(1) For base maps registered within the national framework system, map numbering consists of a zone number and a map sheet number.

(2) Zone numbering refers to the UTM zone number, consisting of three digits, with the first two digits representing the UTM zone number and the last digit representing the location of the TM-3° zone, as listed in Appendix 5.

(3) One TM-3° zone is divided into areas covered by a 1:10,000 scale map with a map face size of 60 cm x 60 cm.

(4) The numbering of 1:10,000 scale map sheets consists of five digits, where the first two digits indicate the column number of the sheet (x-direction) and the next three digits are the row number of the sheet (y-direction) starting from coordinates x = 32,000 m and y = 282,000 m, as listed in Appendix 6.

(5) A 1:10,000 scale map sheet is divided into 16 1:2,500 scale map sheets with a map face size of 60 cm x 60 cm.

(6) The numbering of 1:2,500 scale map sheets consists of seven digits, where the first five digits are the 1:10,000 scale map sheet number and the next two digits are the sequential number of the 1:2,500 scale map sheet within the 1:10,000 scale map sheet, starting from number 1 (one) in the bottom left corner, moving to the right, and then the next row from left to right, as listed in Appendix 7.

(7) The 1:2,500 scale map sheet is divided into nine 1:1,000 scale map sheets with a map face size of 50 cm x 50 cm.

(8) The numbering of 1:1,000 scale map sheets consists of eight digits, where the first seven digits are the 1:2,500 scale map sheet number and the next digit is the sequential number of the 1:1,000 scale map sheet within the 1:2,500 scale map sheet, starting from number 1 (one) in the bottom left corner and continuing as listed in Attachment 7.

(9) For larger scale map sheets (1:500 and 1:250), the map face size is the same as the 1:1,000 scale map face size, and the division and numbering of the map sheets are in accordance with the provisions referred to in paragraph (8), by adding one digit to each smaller scale map sheet number.

(10) An example of the division and numbering of map sheets in the national basic framework system is as listed in Attachment 7.
