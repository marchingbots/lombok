Peraturan Menteri (PM) No. 3 Tahun 1997 tentang KETENTUAN PELAKSANAAN PERATURAN PEMERINTAH NOMOR 24 TAHUN 1997 TENTANG PENDAFTARAN TANAH


Government Regulation (PP) No. 24 of 1997 concerning implementing provisions for government regulation no 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration.


Chapter 2: Measurement and Mapping.

Part 3: Determination and Installation of Land Boundaries
Article section Description Notes
19 1
For the purposes of determining land boundaries as referred to in Article 18 of Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997:
19 1a
the applicant in question in sporadic land registration, or
19 1b
the rights holder of a land plot that has not been registered, or that has been registered but does not yet have a measurement certificate/situational drawing, or that the measurement certificate/situational drawing no longer corresponds to the actual situation, and the party controlling the land plot in question, in systematic land registration, are required to indicate the boundaries of the land plot in question and, if there is an agreement regarding the boundaries with the rights holders of adjacent land plots, to install boundary markers.
19 2
Determination of land boundaries as referred to in paragraph (1) is carried out by the Adjudication Committee in systematic land registration, and by the Head of the Land Office or a Land Office employee assigned by him in sporadic land registration.
19 3
If the survey applicant or land rights holder is unable to attend at the appointed time to show the boundaries of the land plot as referred to in paragraph (1), then the designation of the boundaries may be delegated by written power of attorney to another person.
19 4
If the boundary markers that have been installed do not match the results of the boundary determination as referred to in paragraph (2), the measurement applicant and the relevant rights holder must move the boundary markers in accordance with the boundaries that have been determined.
19 5
The determination of boundaries is outlined in the Legal Data Research and Determination of Boundaries Report (list of contents 201).
19 6
If boundary determination also involves boundary arrangement, the results of the boundary arrangement are set out in the Boundary Arrangement Report (list of contents 201A).
19 7
The boundary arrangement as referred to in paragraph (6) must be approved by the relevant rights holder and this approval must also be stated in the Boundary Arrangement Minutes.
20 1
In the event of a dispute regarding the boundaries of adjacent land plots, the Adjudication Committee in systematic land registration or the Head of the Land Office/appointed surveyor in sporadic land registration shall attempt to resolve the dispute amicably through deliberation between the rights holder and the rights holders of the adjacent land. If successful, the resulting boundary determination shall be set out in a Boundary Dispute Resolution Report (list of contents 200).
20 2
If, at the time of determining the boundaries and measuring the land plot, efforts to resolve the matter peacefully through deliberation are unsuccessful, temporary boundaries will be determined based on the boundaries that are actually the boundaries of the land plot in question as referred to in Article 19 paragraph (1) of Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997, and any party who objects will be notified in writing to file a lawsuit with the Court.
20 3
The steps for determining and measuring temporary boundaries as referred to in paragraph (2) are included in the 201 checklist and recorded on the measurement drawing.
20 4
If the dispute is submitted to the court and the court issues a legally binding decision regarding the land, which is accompanied by a Minutes of Execution or a settlement is reached between the parties before the announcement period as referred to in Article 26 of Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997 ends, then the notes regarding the temporary boundaries on the 201 form and the measurement drawing shall be deleted by crossing them out with black ink.
20 5
Regarding land plots that, according to evidence of ownership, can be registered through recognition of rights in accordance with the provisions of Article 24 paragraph (2) of Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997 or can be granted with certain rights to individuals or legal entities, the boundaries are determined by excluding riverbanks and land designated for roads in accordance with the Detailed Spatial Plan for the relevant area.
20 6
In systematic land registration, state land that will be granted rights to individuals or legal entities and has been measured before the village/sub-district area is designated as the location for systematic land registration, but for which a measurement certificate has not yet been issued, will have its boundaries re-determined by the Adjudication Committee.
21 1
Boundary signs are installed at every corner of the land boundary and, if deemed necessary by officers who carry out measurements also at certain points along the way the boundary line of the land plot.
21 2
For boundary corners that are clearly located because they are marked by permanently installed objects such as concrete fences, wall fences or wire fence reinforcing poles/stakes, boundary markers do not need to be installed.
22 1
If the dispute is submitted to the court and the court issues a legally binding decision regarding the land, which is accompanied by a Minutes of Execution or a settlement is reached between the parties before the announcement period as referred to in Article 26 of Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997 ends, then the notes regarding the temporary boundaries on the 201 form and the measurement drawing shall be deleted by crossing them out with black ink.


Other Notes:
