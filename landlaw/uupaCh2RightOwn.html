1. Undang-Undang No. 5 Tahun 1960 tentang Peraturan Dasar Pokok-Pokok Agraria (UUPA)


Law no. 5 of 1960 concerning Basic Regulations on Agrarian Principles (UUPA)


NOTES


Chapter 2: The Rights on Land, Water, and Air Space, and Land Registration.
The Right of Ownership:


Article 20(1):The right of ownership is hereditary right and is the strongest and fullest right one can have on land that may be possessed by a citizen, considering provision laid down in Article 6.

Article 21(1): Only indonesian citizen may have rights of ownership.

Article 21(2): Corporations which may possess the right of ownership and its requirements shall be determined by government.

Article 21(3): Any foreigner who has obtained a right of ownership thru inheritance, without a will or thru communal marital property AND any Indonesian citizen too, having right to ownership and losing nationality are obliged to relenquish that right within a period of ONE YEAR after the obtaining that right after losing that nationality.

Article 21(4): talks about dual citizenship and loss of ownership right of possessed land.

Article 24; The use of land with the right of ownership by another than the owner shall be restricted and regulated by legislative regulation.

Article 25: right of ownership may be used as a security of a debt by means of a lien (hak tanggungan)

Article 27: the right of ownership is annulled if:
(a) the land falls back to the State (1) because the revocation of the right based on Article 18; (2) because the voluntary transfer by its owner; (3) because the land is lying fallow; (4)because of provisions in Article 21(3), Article 26(2);
(b) the land is destroyed.


Other Notes:

Article 27 annullment of ownership if land is destroyed.
note that use of land by someone other than owner is restricted via govt regulation.
