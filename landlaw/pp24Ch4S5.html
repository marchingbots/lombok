Peraturan Pemerintah (PP) No. 24 Tahun 1997 tentang Pendaftaran Tanah


Government Regulation (PP) No. 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration


NOTES


Chapter 4: FIRST-TIME LAND REGISTRATION.
Section 5: Presentation of Physical Data and Juridical Data.


Article Description Notes
33(1)
For purposes related to the presentation of physical and juridical data, the Land Office shall organize land registration administration (registry), which shall comprise cadastral maps, daftar tanah (land registers), surat ukur (survey documents), buku tanah (land books), and daftar nama (name registers).
.
33(2)
The format of daftar tanah (land registers), surat ukur (survey documents), buku tanah (land books), and daftar nama (name registers) shall be determined by the Minister.
34(1)
Any interested party shall be entitled to knowing the physical data and juridical data contained in cadastral maps, daftar tanah (land registers), surat ukur (survey documents), and buku tanah (land books).
34(2)
The physical data and juridical data contained in daftar nama (name registers) are open only to certain government institutions which require such data to carry out their duties.
34(3)
The requisitions and procedures concerning the acquisition of information or data as meant in paragraphs (1) and (2) shall be determined by the Minister.
