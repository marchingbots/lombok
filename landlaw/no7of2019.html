Peraturan Menteri Agraria dan Tata Ruang/Kepala Badan Pertanahan Nasional Nomor 7 Tahun 2019

Regulation of the Minister of Agrarian Affairs and Spatial Planning/Head of the National Land Agency Number 7 of 2019


CHAPTER LAW NOTES

Menimbang / Meningat


Notes
Menimbang:

a. that the form and content of land title certificates, management rights, waqf land, ownership rights to apartment units, and mortgage rights are regulated in Regulation of the Minister of Agrarian Affairs/Head of the National Land Agency Number 3 of 1997 concerning Implementing Provisions for Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration;

b. that to adapt the form and content of certificates as referred to in letter a to the needs of the community and developments in information technology, Regulation of the Minister of Agrarian Affairs and Spatial Planning/Head of the National Land Agency has been stipulated Number 7 of 2016 concerning the Form and Content of Land Title Certificates;

c. that, taking into account the availability of supporting data, facilities and infrastructure, and information technology, it is necessary to readjust the provisions regarding the form and content of land title certificates as stipulated in the Ministerial Regulation referred to in letter b;

d. that, based on the considerations referred to in letters a, b, and c, it is necessary to stipulate the Regulation of the Minister of Agrarian Affairs and Spatial Planning/Head of the National Land Agency concerning the Second Amendment to Regulation of the Minister of Agrarian Affairs/Head of the National Land Agency Number 3 of 1997 concerning Implementing Provisions of Government Regulation Number 24 of 1997 concerning Land Registration;
