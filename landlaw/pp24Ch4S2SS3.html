Subsection 3: Fixing of Boundaries


Article Description Notes

17(4)

18(1)
The fixing of the boundaries of a land parcel which has been possessed under an unregistered right or under a registered right for which no surat ukurs (survey documents) or gambar situasis (survey drawings) are available or of which the survey document/situation drawing no longer depicts the reality shall be conducted by the Adjudication Committee in the case of systematic registration and by the Head of the land Office in the case of sporadic land registration on the basis of the boundaries which have been located by the holder of the right on the land parcel in question and, where possible, agreed upon by the holders of the rights on the adjacent land parcels.
this is how the boundary is determined. "WHERE POSSIBLE"
18(2)
The fixing of the boundaries of a land parcel on which a new right will be granted shall be conducted in line with the provision as meant in paragraph (1) or on the basis of designation by the authorized institution.
18(3)
In fixing the boundaries of a land parcel, the Head of the Adjudication Committee and the Head of the Land Office shall take into account the boundaries of the already registered land parcel or land parcels and the relevant surat ukurs (survey documents) or gambar situasis (situation drawings).
18(4)
The agreement as meant in paragraphs (1) and (2) shall be stated in an official report which is to be signed by those who have provided the agreement.
agreement?
18(5)
The format of the official report as meant in paragraph (4) shall be determined by the Minister.
19(1)
In the case where the fixing of the boundaries of a land parcel as meant in Article 18(1) is not agreed upon by the holder of the right on the land parcel in question and the holders of the rights on the adjacent land parcels, the surveying of the land parcel in question shall be conducted for provisional use on the basis of the boundaries which in reality have been the boundaries of the land parcel in question.
disagreement on fixed boundaries - surveying is provisional"

19(3)
The provisional surveying of a land parcel as meant in paragraphs (1) and (2), including the fact that the fixed boundaries have not been agreed upon or that the land-right holder has failed to present himself/herself shall be stated in an official report which is to be signed by the Head of the Adjudication Committee in the case of systematic land registration or by the Head of the relevant Land Office in the case of sporadic land registration.
19(4)
To the relevant gambar ukur (survey drawing) resulting from provisional survey as meant in paragraph (3), a note or mark shall be added which shows that the boundaries of the land parcel in question are provisional boundaries.

19(5)

19A(1)
The installation of boundary signs is carried out by the applicant after obtaining the approval of the adjacent landowner

19A(2)
In order to install boundary markers as referred to in paragraph (1), photographs of the installed boundary markers must be taken, accompanied by information about the location, coordinates or geotagging.

19A(3)
The installation of boundary markers as referred to in paragraph (1) and their maintenance are the responsibility of the applicant.
19A(4)
The installation of boundary markers as referred to in paragraph (1) is set out in a Statement Letter regarding the Installation of Boundary Marks and the Approval of the Owners of the Bordering Land.
boundary letter
19A(5)
The photographic results of the boundary markers as referred to in paragraph (2) and the Statement Letter regarding the Installation of Boundary Marks and Approval of the Owners of the Bordering Land as referred to in paragraph (4) are required to complete the application documents.
19A(6)
The Statement of Boundary Mark Installation and Approval of the Owner of the Bordered Land must be made in accordance with the format set out in Attachment I, which is an integral part of this Ministerial Regulation
19B(1)
Boundary determination is carried out by a surveyor based on the Boundary Sign Installation Statement and the Approval of the Owners of the Borders.
19B(2)
Determination of boundaries is carried out at the location of the land plot to be measured with the following provisions:
19B(2)(a)

19B(2)(b)
the applicant indicates the boundaries of the land plot requested.
19B(3)
If the boundary determination is carried out simultaneously with the boundary arrangement, the results of the boundary arrangement are set out in the Boundary Arrangement Report (d.i. 201A) which is approved by the relevant Rights Holder and the adjacent landowner.
Can happen same day as measuring
19C(1)
Boundary designation and boundary determination activities can utilize information and communication technology that takes into account cross-aspects of space and time, as well as interactive digital communication, such as Augmented Reality/Virtual Reality (AR/VR) technology, video call/audiovisual communication applications, or other technologies.
owners can be on the phone with video
19C(2)
The results of boundary designation and boundary determination activities as referred to in paragraph (1) are in the form of an Electronic Document recording.
19D(1)
In the case of above ground and/or underground spaces, boundary determination is carried out by a measuring officer based on a designation from the development actor in accordance with the physical boundaries of the building that has been constructed and verified against a 3 (three) dimensional model of the building that has been constructed.
19D(2)
The 3-dimensional model referred to in paragraph (1) can be created by converting 2-dimensional as-built drawings, mapping results using a laser scanner, or photogrammetric mapping results in a 3-dimensional model format presented using one of the following formats: DWG, DXF, IFC, OBJ, RVT, CityGML, or other 3-dimensional presentation formats.
